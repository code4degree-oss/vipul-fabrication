About Vipul Fabrication

Trusted Industrial Fabrication Partner in Bhosari

Located in Indrayani Nagar, Bhosari, Pune, Vipul Fabrication has been delivering dependable Mild Steel (MS) and Stainless Steel (SS) fabrication solutions since 2010.

With over a decade and a half of hands-on experience, we cater to factories, processing units, industrial warehouses, contractors, and local businesses in the Pimpri-Chinchwad & Pune industrial belt. From building high-span factory sheds and precision fluid pipelines to heavy-duty storage tanks and tall exhaust chimneys, we take pride in rigid construction, clean weld joints, and strict compliance with safety norms.
